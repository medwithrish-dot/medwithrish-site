# Interview university catalogue: sources and timing decisions

Checked 6 September 2026. The runnable catalogue lives in `app/phloemai/interviews/_data/universities.ts` and covers the user's 42 named universities and awarding combinations. It is not intended to assert that the list contains every current UK admissions route. Admissions details can change between cycles and between home, international and graduate-entry routes; the candidate's invitation takes precedence.

TheUKCATPeople was the requested starting source. Its current school profiles were opened and their interview sections reviewed. Several pages contain older examples beneath a newer format summary; the catalogue uses the current summary, and explicitly labels unconfirmed figures. We did not copy the site's question bank. Prompts in this platform are original practice material, not claims about actual university questions.

`stationCount`, `stationSeconds`, `preparationSeconds` and `breakSeconds` are executable **rehearsal settings**. `published` means the source reports the core station timing; the note still explains omitted or unreported components. `practice` means the preset contains a chosen count, an estimate, an adapted block, or an interval whose length the source does not publish. Never display a practice preset as an officially verified circuit. Reading time and rest time are separate fields; do not count a reading period twice as a rest interval. A panel or mixed-format block is not automatically an MMI station.

## Findings that affect the experience

- Manchester matches the supplied example: five stations, eight minutes each, two-minute intervals. The current [TheUKCATPeople Manchester summary](https://www.theukcatpeople.co.uk/guide/medical-school/medicine/university-of-manchester) differs from older timings lower on the same page.
- Keele uses two online 15-minute panels according to the [current Keele guide](https://www.theukcatpeople.co.uk/guide/medical-school/medicine/university-of-keele). Its separation into two interviews is preserved; the short interval in this platform is a rehearsal choice.
- Dundee's standard undergraduate interview now uses a group discussion and an assessor discussion. [Dundee's official interview page](https://www.dundee.ac.uk/undergraduate/medicine/interview) confirms the initial discussion is around 30 minutes and the overall event includes registration and a welcome. Two 30-minute blocks are a practice approximation; the second duration is not confirmed. This must not be confused with ScotGEM.
- The [official ScotGEM selection page](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/medicine/scotgem-mbchb/selection/) describes seven-minute MMI stations and 30-second breaks, without specifying the total station count. The chosen six-station rehearsal is labelled practice.
- The [University of London federation](https://www.london.ac.uk/federation) is an awarding/federation entry in the user's list, not a single school-level admissions interview. Its entry directs students to the relevant member school and only launches general practice.
- Southampton, Dundee, Kent and Medway, Hull York and other assessment-day formats include group or other tasks that a solo conversation cannot reproduce. Their timing notes explain the scope and link to Groups for peer practice.

## Product reference

[MedPrepPartner's public site](https://med.preppartner.ai/) describes spoken interview answers, natural follow-up questions, per-station feedback and timed MMI circuits. These interaction ideas informed the experience. Its public description is not evidence that gaze or fidget analysis is required. No proprietary UI, code, questions or scoring rules were copied, and no account access was used.
