Read the assumptions with the numbers

These are scenarios, not measured production usage or quotes. Output-token allowances must include all billable reasoning tokens; a long thinking response will cost more. Final transcription is billed once per recording in this scenario. Live conversational voice, streaming transcription, dynamic interview follow-up text, batch/cache discounts and one-time library narration are not included. For an adaptive interview, increase the feedback count or model its additional calls separately.

The infrastructure allowance must be reviewed as traffic and retained recordings grow. Cached playback still uses storage and bandwidth. Tax, payment fees, support, content work, marketing and other business costs are excluded. No quality ranking is implied by model prices; compare feedback with human marking before switching.

Formula: monthly AI = users × (feedback + diagnostics + transcription + new voice costs per user) × exchange rate × (1 + contingency). Total = monthly AI + infrastructure. Published provider prices were checked on 8 September 2026; recheck them before budgeting.
