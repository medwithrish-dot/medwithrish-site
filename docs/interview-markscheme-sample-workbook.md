# Interview question markscheme sample workbook

This is a deliberately small, stratified sample of **18 questions** from the live interview question bank. It covers all eight categories, a spread of subcategories and every difficulty level that exists in each sampled area.

## How to use this with Codex

1. Write beneath each heading you want to mark.
2. Leave questions you do not want to handle yet blank.
3. Paste the completed sections back into chat. Keep the question ID so the markscheme can be attached to the correct live question.
4. For a visual question, provide the image (or a precise table of its values) and complete the visual answer key. A fair question-specific markscheme cannot be made without the final visual.

Use short bullet points. You do not need polished prose. The fields map to the product's **General / Start / Middle / End** self-review format.

## Visual/source flags

- **[IMAGE REQUIRED]** `iq-18-001-data-stations` — needs the final graph.
- **[IMAGE REQUIRED]** `iq-18-006-graphs-and-trends` — needs the final graph, including axes, scale, labels and source note.
- **[SOURCE TEXT REQUIRED]** `iq-18-013-article-analysis` — needs the study extract or article summary. This may be text rather than an image.

Do not create a final markscheme for one of these flagged questions until its asset is fixed. Otherwise the key could reward claims that are not supported by what the candidate sees.
