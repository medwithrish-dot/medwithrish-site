iq-01-001-motivation-for-medicine
Personal & Motivation / Motivation for Medicine
Question: What has influenced your decision to pursue a career in medicine?
GENERAL
- Genuine and personal motivation
- Realistic understanding of medicine
- Uses Situation, Task, Action, Result and Reflection naturally, without reciting the framework
- Reflects on experiences rather than simply listing them

START
- Directly answers what influenced them
- Introduces a meaningful personal experience or reason
- Avoids a generic opening

MIDDLE
- Uses one or two meaningful experiences
- Explains what they learnt from each experience
- Links the learning specifically to medicine
- Shows awareness of both rewarding and challenging aspects of medicine

END
- Explains how these experiences strengthened their motivation
- Shows realistic commitment to medicine
- Ends with reflection rather than repeating earlier points

MISTAKES
- Listing experiences without reflection
- Giving reasons that could apply to any career
- Idealising medicine
- Focusing mainly on money, status or prestige
