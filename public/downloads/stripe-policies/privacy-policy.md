# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 11 October 2026

MedWithRish (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides medical and dental admissions resources, tutoring, interview practice, guides and related digital services.

## Who we are

The service is operated by MedWithRish.

Contact: medwithrish@gmail.com  

## Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, account details, payment and subscription information, messages you send us, tutoring or admissions preferences, and technical information such as device, browser, IP address and usage data.

Payment card details are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our services, create and manage accounts, process payments and subscriptions, provide customer support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. We may send service-related messages. We only send marketing where permitted by law, and you can opt out at any time.

## Legal bases

Where UK data protection law applies, we process information because it is necessary to perform a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, for our legitimate interests, or because you have given consent.

## Sharing information

We share information only where reasonably necessary with service providers such as Stripe for payments, Supabase for account and database hosting, hosting and analytics providers, professional advisers, and public authorities where legally required. Providers process data under their own terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

## Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including accounting, legal and dispute-resolution requirements. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your information, and withdraw consent. Contact us at medwithrish@gmail.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or your local data-protection authority.

## Cookies

We may use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep accounts secure and make the service work. Where required, we will request consent for non-essential cookies.

## Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new “last updated” date.
