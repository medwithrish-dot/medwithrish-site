# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** 11 October 2026

## Subscriptions

You may cancel a subscription at any time through the Stripe customer billing portal or by emailing medwithrish@gmail.com. Cancellation stops future renewals. Unless required by law or stated at checkout, the current paid billing period remains active and is not automatically refunded.

## Digital products

Because digital content may be supplied immediately, you may lose a statutory cancellation right once delivery begins if you expressly agreed to immediate access. This does not affect rights relating to faulty, misdescribed or inaccessible content. Contact us promptly if there is a problem and we will work to fix it or provide an appropriate remedy.

## Tutoring sessions

Please request cancellation or rescheduling by email as soon as possible. A full refund is generally available when requested at least 24 hours before a session. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be non-refundable unless the issue was caused by us or consumer law requires otherwise.

## How to request a refund

Email medwithrish@gmail.com with your name, purchase email, order or subscription details, and the reason for your request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe. Processing times depend on your bank or card provider.

Nothing in this policy limits your mandatory consumer rights.
